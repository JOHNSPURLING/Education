## At a glance

- Part three of KCSIE sets out safer recruitment. Some elements are legal requirements (the checks and the single central record); others are 'should' guidance (how you advertise, shortlist and interview).
- Schools **must** keep a **single central record (SCR)** of pre-appointment checks for all staff, including supply staff and teacher trainees on salaried routes; academies and independent schools must also record members of the proprietor body.
- At least one person on every interview panel should have completed safer recruitment training (a legal requirement for maintained schools).
- **2026 change:** the Crime and Policing Act 2026 removes the 'supervision' exemption from the definition of regulated activity. Volunteers who teach, train, instruct or supervise children frequently are in regulated activity even when supervised, so barred-list checks apply.
- The check is only part of the picture: a deterrent culture, scrutiny of employment history and good induction matter as much.

## Status and current version

KCSIE 2026, Part three, in force from 1 September 2026. It applies to all schools and colleges; the underlying legal requirements differ slightly by school type (School Staffing (England) Regulations 2009 for maintained schools; the independent school standards for academies and independent schools).

**What changed in 2026** (reported by NSPCC, legal and local authority briefings; confirm against the text):

- The definition of **regulated activity** is revised to reflect the removal of the supervision exemption. The frequency test remains (commonly summarised as more than three days in a 30-day period, or overnight). The DBS decision flowchart in earlier editions has been removed. Not verified: the exact commencement date of the statutory change; check before changing practice for existing volunteers, and take advice from your HR provider.
- Candidates should be told that **online searches** may be carried out at shortlisting (online searches themselves have been in KCSIE since 2022).
- Not verified: any other 2026 changes to Part three; see the summary-of-changes annex.

## What schools must do

**Checks before appointment** (staff in regulated activity):

- identity;
- enhanced DBS certificate with children's barred list information;
- a separate barred list check if the person starts before the certificate arrives (with supervision);
- prohibition from teaching check for those doing teaching work;
- section 128 direction check for management roles in academies and independent schools, and for governors in maintained schools;
- mental and physical fitness for the role;
- right to work in the UK;
- overseas checks where the person has lived or worked outside the UK;
- professional qualifications where required.

**The SCR must** record, for each person, whether each required check was done and the date it was completed or the certificate obtained. For agency and third-party staff, schools must obtain written confirmation from the employer that checks have been done and check the person's identity on arrival.

**Schools should** also: include a safeguarding statement in adverts; use application forms, not CVs alone; require a self-declaration from shortlisted candidates; examine gaps and inconsistencies; obtain references before interview, directly from a senior person at the current employer, and verify them; ask safeguarding questions at interview; consider an online search on shortlisted candidates.

**Governors and volunteers.** Maintained school governors must have an enhanced DBS check; academy trustees and members need the checks set by the independent school standards. Volunteers need a written risk assessment where no check is legally required; schools may not request barred-list information for someone not in regulated activity.

## In practice

- **Staff involved in recruitment:** use the school's templates; record who checked what and when; never accept open references or references from candidates' hands.
- **DSL and HR/business manager:** audit the SCR termly against the staff list, supply bookings, the governor list and the volunteer list. In autumn 2026, review every volunteer role against the revised regulated activity definition and record the decision.
- **Contractors:** agree checks in the contract; identity is checked on arrival; unchecked contractors are supervised and not left alone with pupils.
- **Alternative provision and work experience:** obtain written confirmation that the provider has completed appropriate checks on its staff.
- **Governors and trustees:** the safeguarding governor should sample the SCR with the business manager at least annually and look at two or three personnel files end to end.

## Common pitfalls

- Blank cells or 'N/A' on the SCR with no explanation; dates of the check confused with dates of the certificate.
- No section 128 check for governors or senior leaders; no prohibition check for instructors and cover supervisors who teach.
- Missing overseas checks, or missing evidence of the decision when one could not be obtained.
- Agency confirmation letters filed but identity not checked on the day.
- Keeping copies of DBS certificates longer than needed (data protection guidance is no longer than six months).
- Supervised volunteers still treated as outside regulated activity after the 2026 change.
- References received after the start date, or not followed up when vague.
